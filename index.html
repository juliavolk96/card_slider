Jinshi

A high-ranking and extraordinarily beautiful eunuch who manages the Inner Court. Possessing a sharp mind and hidden ambitions, his true identity is shrouded in mystery.

Lishu

The young and timid Virtuous Consort of the Crystal Pavilion. Despite her high status, she is often misunderstood due to her severe food allergies.

Xiaolan

A hardworking maidservant who works in the palace's laundry. She is Maomao's most reliable source of information and rumors among servants.

Maomao

The pragmatic daughter of an apothecary. She uses her vast knowledge of poisons to solve palace mysteries while avoiding court politics.

Gyokuyou

One of the Emperor's favored high-ranking consorts. A wise woman who recognizes Maomao's genius and enlists her help in the Jade Pavilion.
